Professional Bio

I am a software development engineer (SDE) and a graduate student in artificial intelligence and machine learning at Indiana Wesleyan University. My engineering background is in building and shipping production software, where reliability, clear interfaces, and measurable results are the standard. In this degree program I am deepening the other half of the craft: how models are selected, trained, evaluated, and deployed responsibly.

My coursework so far spans data analytics foundations, data visualization, big data systems, machine learning fundamentals, and large language models and generative AI. In AIML-501 (Model Development), my focus is the model layer itself: how algorithms turn data into predictions, how to choose among them, and how to explain their behavior to the people who depend on them.

Personal Value Proposition

I bring an engineer's discipline to machine learning: I build models the way I build software — interpretable where possible, evaluated against honest metrics, and designed to survive contact with real users and real data. My value is the combination: production software experience plus a growing, formally trained command of model development, so the AI systems I work on are not only clever, but dependable, explainable, and responsibly deployed.
